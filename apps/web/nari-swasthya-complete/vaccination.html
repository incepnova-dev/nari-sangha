Why: reduces risk of cervical cancer

Pregnancy timeline

Clear trimester-based guidance with safe/unsafe notes.

Before pregnancy

  • Confirm immunity: MMR, Varicella (if missing, vaccinate before conception)
  • Consider HPV if eligible and not completed

During pregnancy

  • Flu: any trimester (seasonal)
  • Tdap: 27–36 weeks
  • COVID-19: per recommendation

Live vaccines (MMR/Varicella) are not given during pregnancy.

Postpartum

  • Catch-up vaccines if missed
  • Breastfeeding-safe guidance from your clinician

What to bring

Any past vaccine records, pregnancy card, current medications list.

Common mild effects

Arm soreness, fatigue, mild fever — typically 1–2 days.

Where to get vaccinated

Clinic, hospital, pharmacy, community vaccination drives.
