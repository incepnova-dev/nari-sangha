Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

Safe motherhood intervention promoting institutional delivery with cash assistance

Gov. Hospital Cash Aid ASHA Help

What you get

  • Rural Mothers: ₹1,400 cash assistance for institutional birth
  • Urban Mothers: ₹1,000 cash assistance for institutional birth
  • BPL Mothers: ₹500 for home delivery (19+ years)
  • ASHA Support: Escort to hospital & facilitated admission
  • Post-delivery: Cash is paid at the center itself

Who it helps

All pregnant women (LPA states)
BPL/SC/ST women (HPA states)
19 years of age and above

Bring these

Jadav / MCP Card Aadhaar Card Bank Account Details BPL Card (if applicable)

60‑second path

  1. 1 Register with ASHA/ANM
  2. 2 Get at least 3 ANC checkups
  3. 3 Deliver at Govt Facility
  4. 4 Receive Bank Transfer
