Before you book: checklist

Use this before Teleconsultation or Find Specialists.

What to verify

  • Clinic/doctor is in-network
  • Referral needed? (common in HMOs)
  • Teleconsultation coverage (video visits)
  • Lab/ultrasound coverage and location
  • Maternity coverage details (prenatal, delivery)
  • Preventive screening coverage (Pap, mammogram)

What to ask (copy/paste)

1) Are you in-network for my plan and tier?
2) What is my estimated patient responsibility for this visit?
3) Do you bill as “office visit” or “telehealth”?
4) Are labs/imaging billed separately?
5) Do you need prior authorization or referral?
