Pregnancy Trimester Guide

Understanding what to expect during each stage of your pregnancy journey.

First Trimester (1-12 weeks)

  • Week 1-4: Fertilization and implantation
  • Week 5-8: Major organs begin forming
  • Week 9-12: Baby's heartbeat detectable

Common symptoms: Morning sickness, fatigue, breast tenderness

Key actions: Prenatal vitamins, first ultrasound, blood tests

Second Trimester (13-26 weeks)

  • Week 13-16: Baby can hear sounds
  • Week 17-20: Feel baby's movements
  • Week 21-26: Rapid brain development

Common symptoms: Reduced nausea, energy boost, growing belly

Key actions: Anatomy scan, glucose screening, childbirth classes

Third Trimester (27-40 weeks)

  • Week 27-32: Eyes open, baby gains weight
  • Week 33-36: Bones harden, lungs mature
  • Week 37-40: Full term, ready for birth

Common symptoms: Back pain, frequent urination, Braxton Hicks

Key actions: Hospital bag, birth plan, Group B strep test

Essential Pregnancy Nutrition

Nourishing yourself and your baby with the right nutrients.

Folic Acid

400-800 mcg daily prevents neural tube defects. Start before conception.

Calcium

1000 mg daily for baby's bones and teeth. Found in dairy, leafy greens.

Iron

27 mg daily prevents anemia. Red meat, beans, fortified cereals.

DHA (Omega-3)

200-300 mg daily for brain development. Fish, walnuts, supplements.

Vitamin D

600 IU daily for calcium absorption. Sunlight, fortified milk, supplements.

Protein

71g daily for tissue growth. Lean meat, eggs, legumes, nuts.

Prenatal Care Schedule

Regular checkups ensure healthy development and early detection of any concerns.

First Visit (6-8 weeks)

Complete health history, blood tests, urine tests, Rh factor, confirm pregnancy, estimate due date

Monthly Visits (Weeks 8-28)

Weight, blood pressure, urine protein/glucose, fetal heartbeat, fundal height measurement

Bi-weekly (Weeks 28-36)

Increased monitoring, glucose screening, ultrasounds, discuss birth plan, warning signs

Weekly (Weeks 36-40)

Cervical checks, baby position, Group B strep test, final preparations for delivery

Common Pregnancy Concerns

When to call your healthcare provider.

Call Immediately If You Experience:

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Severe abdominal pain or cramping
  • Sudden decrease in baby's movements
  • Signs of preterm labor (contractions before 37 weeks)
  • Severe headache with vision changes
  • Fluid leaking from vagina
  • High fever above 101°F (38.3°C)

Normal Pregnancy Symptoms:

  • Mild cramping and spotting (early pregnancy)
  • Morning sickness and food aversions
  • Fatigue and mood swings
  • Breast tenderness and growth
  • Frequent urination
  • Mild swelling in feet and ankles
  • Braxton Hicks contractions (third trimester)

Pregnancy Wellness Tips

Self-care practices for a healthy pregnancy.

Stay Active

30 minutes of moderate exercise most days. Walking, swimming, prenatal yoga are excellent choices.

Rest Well

7-9 hours of sleep. Use pregnancy pillows, sleep on your left side for better circulation.

Hydrate

8-10 glasses of water daily. Prevents constipation, reduces swelling, maintains amniotic fluid.

Manage Stress

Meditation, prenatal massage, breathing exercises. Join support groups for emotional wellness.

Avoid Harmful Substances

No smoking, alcohol, or recreational drugs. Limit caffeine to 200mg per day.

Educate Yourself

Take childbirth classes, read about labor and delivery, prepare a birth plan.
