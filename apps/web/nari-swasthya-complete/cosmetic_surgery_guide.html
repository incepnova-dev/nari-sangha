Recovery Timeline

Understanding what to expect during your healing journey

Days 1-7: Immediate Post-Operative

Rest is crucial. Arrange help for daily tasks, wear compression garments as directed, take prescribed medications, and watch for any signs of infection. Gentle walking is encouraged to prevent blood clots.

Weeks 2-6: Early Recovery

Gradually increase activity levels. Light walking can progress to normal daily activities. Avoid heavy lifting (over 5kg). Begin scar treatments when approved. Swelling peaks around week 2-3.

Months 2-3: Returning to Normal

Resume exercise gradually, starting with low-impact activities. Most patients can return to full activities by month 3-6. Continue scar care and sun protection.

Months 6-12: Final Results

Results become fully visible by 6 months, with final settling at 12-18 months. Scars continue to fade and soften. Maintain stable weight and healthy lifestyle for longevity of results.
